Privacy Policy for HabitFlow

Last updated: 21 August 2026

HabitFlow is a habit and water tracker for Android, published by Mihary Joel ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

HabitFlow has no accounts and no servers of our own. Everything you record — your habits, your water log, your streak, your XP and gems — is stored only on your own phone. We never receive it, and we cannot read it. The one exception is advertising: HabitFlow shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects data to serve those ads.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, using your device's app storage. It is not transmitted anywhere:

  • The habits you create (name and icon) and which days you completed them
  • Your water log entries and your daily water goal
  • Your streak, longest streak, streak freezes, XP, gems and unlocked achievements
  • Your settings, such as your daily goal size and reminder times

You can erase all of it at any time from Settings → Erase all my data, or by uninstalling the app. Because this data never leaves your phone, uninstalling removes it permanently and there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for a name, email address, phone number or account. We do not use analytics or crash reporting. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, or health records, and the app requests no permission to do so.

Advertising

HabitFlow is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and language
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interaction data, such as whether an ad was shown, viewed or tapped

Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services here, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, HabitFlow shows you Google's consent form the first time you open it, and requests no ad at all until you have made a choice. You can change that choice later from Settings → Ad privacy options. We also ask Google to limit ads to the family-friendly "G" content rating.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, HabitFlow schedules notifications locally on your device at the times shown in Settings. There is no push server involved: no notification token is created, sent or stored anywhere. You can turn reminders off in the app, or revoke the notification permission in your Android settings.

Children

HabitFlow is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Deleting your HabitFlow data

HabitFlow, published by Mihary Joel, stores everything only on your own device — we have no account system and no server copy to delete on our end. To delete your data:

  • In the app, open Settings → Erase all my data. This immediately and permanently deletes every habit, water log entry, streak, XP, gem and achievement stored on your phone.
  • Uninstalling the app has the same effect, since nothing is kept anywhere else.
  • For any other request, or if you would prefer we confirm a deletion for you, email miharyjoel@gmail.com.

What this deletes: all app data listed under "Information stored on your device" above, with no retention period — it is gone as soon as you erase it or uninstall.

What this does not affect: the advertising ID and ad-interaction data that Google AdMob processes (see Advertising below) are controlled by Google, not by HabitFlow. Reset or delete your advertising ID separately in your Android settings under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. Your control over your data is direct: erase it in the app, reset your advertising ID in Android, or change your ad consent choice at any time.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the page and the "last updated" date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
